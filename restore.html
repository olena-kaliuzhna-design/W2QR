Open Wallet

Load your encrypted QR data to get started. The format will be detected automatically.

Scan with Camera

Use your device camera to scan a printed or on-screen W2QR code.

 Scan with Mobile

Use your phone to scan a QR code or enter a seed phrase, then transfer securely to this device via encrypted bridge.

 Upload File

Drag and drop or browse for a QR code image (PNG, JPG, SVG), a text backup file (JSON, TXT), or a .w2qr encrypted backup. Supports multiple files at once.

 Restore from Google Drive

Sign in with your Google account to retrieve an encrypted backup saved to your Drive. Your password never leaves this device.

 Create New

Generate a new seed phrase and encrypt it as a QR code for secure storage.

 Import Seed Phrase

Already have a seed phrase? Enter it directly to unlock your wallet without scanning a QR code.
