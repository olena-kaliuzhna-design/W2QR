Password tips

  • Use a mnemonic phrase — a sentence you can easily remember but is hard to guess
  • Avoid common words, names, dates, or patterns like "123456" or "qwerty"
  • Longer is stronger — each additional word multiplies the guessing difficulty
  • Mix in unusual characters or substitutions to resist dictionary attacks
  • Never reuse a password from another site or wallet
